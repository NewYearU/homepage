Jie Wang, MD, PhD

Critical Care Medicine • Echocardiography • Artificial Intelligence

I am a physician-scientist specializing in critical care medicine. My research focuses on echocardiographic phenotyping, circulatory physiology, artificial intelligence in cardiovascular imaging, and sepsis-induced organ dysfunction. My goal is to bridge bedside physiology with computational methods to improve precision medicine in critically ill patients.
