Jie Wang, MD

Critical Care Medicine • Echocardiography • Artificial Intelligence

I am a physician-scientist in critical care medicine. My research focuses on echocardiographic phenotyping, hemodynamic monitoring, macro–micro circulatory coherence, artificial intelligence in cardiovascular imaging, and sepsis-induced organ dysfunction. My goal is to bridge bedside physiology with computational methods to improve risk stratification and precision medicine in critically ill patients.
