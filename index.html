Jie Wang, MD, PhD Candidate

Critical Care Medicine • Echocardiography • Artificial Intelligence

I am a physician-scientist in critical care medicine with research interests in echocardiographic phenotyping, circulatory physiology, artificial intelligence in cardiovascular imaging, and sepsis-induced organ dysfunction. My work aims to bridge bedside physiology with computational methods to improve risk stratification and precision medicine in critically ill patients.
